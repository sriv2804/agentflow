# Refund policy

1. **Delivered orders** can be refunded within **30 days of delivery**. After 30 days, refunds are declined.
2. **Late orders**: an order that has not arrived **5 or more days after its expected delivery date** can be refunded in full, even though it was never delivered.
3. The refund amount is **at most the order total**. Refunds above **$200** must be escalated to a supervisor and are not initiated by support agents.
4. Refunds go to the **original payment method** unless the customer asks for store credit.
5. Refunds arrive in **5 to 7 business days** after they are initiated.
6. After a refund is initiated, the order's support ticket is marked **resolved**.
